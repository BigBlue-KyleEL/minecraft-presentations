Secure your supplies

The world has to be converted to cross over, and the conversion is not perfect. This part needs you to actually do something, so read it twice.

Put everything you care about into a chest before the crossing.

Builds survive. Chests and everything inside them survive. Your personal inventory does not. Neither does anything alive — named pets, horses in your stable, the villagers you have been trading with. Those we lose. I would rather tell you now than let you find out on the other side.

The cost of the crossing

Redstone farms may break

The two editions handle redstone and mob spawning differently. If you have a finely tuned farm, assume it needs repairing afterwards.

Combat feels different

A short cooldown between sword swings instead of spam-clicking. A day to adjust, then you stop noticing.

We update late, together

Switch off auto-update for Minecraft on your device. When a big Minecraft update lands we won't get it on day one — I update the server and the crossplay bridge first, then we all move across at once. So new content reaches us a little behind everyone else, rather than half of us being locked out while the other half updated.

Marketplace purchases don't come with us

Paid worlds and packs stay on the Marketplace side. Skins are fine.

If the server is down, we are down

A Realm is Microsoft's problem. This one is mine, and I am the one you will be complaining to.
